## Platform

web

## Stack

Single-file static page (HTML5 + modern CSS + vanilla JS), per the user's brief ("código completo e executável ... em um único arquivo"), deployable to any static host. Earlier in the session the user leaned toward a framework (Astro/Next); the final brief asked for one runnable file, so that wins. Can be ported into Astro later without redesign.

## Users

International and Brazilian travellers in Rio de Janeiro, mostly on a phone, often in a hotel or on the beach, deciding which favela tour to book today or tomorrow. They need to trust that the visit is safe, respectful and genuinely local, pick a tour and time, and book (or ask a quick question on WhatsApp).

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for **Favela Walking Tour**, a licensed tourism agency running guided walks in Rocinha, Rio de Janeiro. The page centralises every tour, its times, price and booking link, and pushes the visitor to book direct (not via resellers). Success = taps on "Reservar" / WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Operating in Rocinha since 2002 with local guides, booked direct with the agency itself (Linktree: "Avoid resellers and save up to 25%"), and tied to a social project that has helped 10,000+ children in the region through sport-based education.

## Operating Context

- Booking happens on the agency's own Checkfront-style booking page: `https://www.favelawalkingtour.com.br/booking-now/#id=<tour-slug>`.
- Quick questions and bookings also via WhatsApp: +55 21 98178-9239 (`https://wa.me/5521981789239`).
- Promo code shown on the official site: `favelawalkingtour`. Student discount 10%.
- Tours run in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Danish and Hebrew.

## Product Principles

1. Book direct: every section ends one tap from a real booking link or WhatsApp.
2. Trust before persuasion: safety, licence, local guides and real ratings sit next to every price.
3. Respect the community: the favela is people's home, never a spectacle; copy and imagery reflect that.
4. Phone first: a visitor on mobile data in Rio must get the whole offer in seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Multilingual audience: Portuguese UI with English tour names kept as the agency publishes them. Note mobility constraints (steps, motorbike tour) honestly.
