Scope: index.html, the single-page marketing site for Favela Walking Tour. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience & job: travellers in Rio, mostly on a phone, pick a tour and time and book direct (or ask on WhatsApp). Proof on hand: 4.9/5, Travelers' Choice, since 2002, real GetYourGuide quotes (Roberto, Bruno), the social football project. Constraints: no invented prices, meeting points, night-life content or reviews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a field map of Rocinha. Every tour is a route drawn across the hill's contour lines, with times as departures and prices as waypoint stamps. It refuses the category default: a drone photo behind a centred headline with three identical cards underneath.

OWN-WORLD: Topographic field-map grammar. Sun-bleached map paper ground, contour lines in brick-earth, forest green fields for the Tijuca/Dois Irmãos flank, route ink in a hot signal orange, sunset gold for the dusk route. Night mode swaps the paper for deep earth-navy with gold window lights. Archivo variable throughout: expanded black for display, condensed tracked caps for map labels set along the terrain, tabular numerals for times.

STORY: The visitor sees where they will walk, sees when and for how much, trusts the guides and community, then taps Reservar or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed SVG contour map of the hill with landmarks (Dois Irmãos, Estrada da Gávea, Passarela, São Conrado). The headline sits large on the left. On the right, a map legend lists the three routes as selectable rows (time, price, Reservar). The primary CTA "Reservar agora" plus WhatsApp sits under the headline, with the proof strip below that.

FORM: Mapa dos Becos, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 47e56735. Signature interaction: selecting a route in the legend draws its path (stroke animation), dims the others and, for the 16:30 route, turns the map to night.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
